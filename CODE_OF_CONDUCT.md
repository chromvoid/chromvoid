# Code of Conduct

ChromVoid is built around security, privacy, and threat-model clarity. Project
discussion should stay direct, technical, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and threat models.
- Keep criticism focused on code, design, documentation, behavior, or evidence.
- Be precise when discussing security claims, limitations, and risk.
- Avoid sharing private information, secrets, credentials, or sensitive user
  data in public channels.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Public vulnerability disclosure before coordinated disclosure is complete.
- Spam, impersonation, or intentionally disruptive participation.

## Reporting

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately at legal@chromvoid.com.
Reports should include the relevant link or context and any information needed
to understand the concern.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, restrict participation, or take
other moderation action when needed to protect the project and community.
